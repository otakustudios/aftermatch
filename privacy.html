Privacy Policy

Aftermatch for iOS · Last updated 4 October 2026

Aftermatch is a family of iOS companion apps that show match statistics and analysis for the gamertags you choose to add. This policy covers every Aftermatch app. They are built to keep everything on your device.

What the apps collect

Nothing. Aftermatch apps have no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and they operate no servers of their own. We do not receive any information about you or your use of the apps.

What leaves your device

When you add a player, the gamertag you enter and the platform you pick are sent over an encrypted connection to the official developer API of the game that app covers, to look up that player's publicly available statistics. For the PUBG: Battlegrounds app that is the PUBG Developer API, operated by KRAFTON, Inc. The app then downloads that player's public match records and telemetry files from the publisher's servers to analyse them. The publisher's own privacy policy governs how it handles those requests; we have no access to them.

What stays on your device

  • The gamertags you add and which one is active
  • Cached API responses, kept briefly so the app stays fast and within the API's limits
  • A compact summary of each analysed match, including the names of other players who appeared in it, taken from the public match record
  • Your filter and sorting preferences

All of this lives inside the app's private container. It is included in your device backups if you use them, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

The PUBG: Battlegrounds app

The first Aftermatch app covers PUBG: Battlegrounds on Steam, Xbox and PlayStation.

My gamertag isn't found. Names are case-sensitive and must match your in-game name exactly. Pick the platform you play on: Steam covers PC, and Xbox and PlayStation each have their own.

It says "try again" or stats won't refresh. The game's API allows a small number of requests per minute. The app caches what it fetches, so this is rare; if it happens, wait a minute and pull to refresh.

Analysis only shows the last two weeks. PUBG publishes roughly 14 days of matches. Aftermatch keeps a summary of every match it analyses on your phone, so your history grows from the day you install. Open the app at least every couple of weeks and nothing is missed.

Some analyses are locked. Each one needs a minimum amount of data before its numbers mean anything. The Your data page shows how far along each is.

Where is my data? On your device only. See the privacy policy.
